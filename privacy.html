Privacy Policy

Triton Property Group LLC  ·  Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Triton Property Group LLC ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information you provide when communicating with us about rental properties, including via SMS text messaging.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you inquire about or lease a property from us:

  • Name, email address, and phone number
  • Rental application information (employment, income, rental history)
  • Communications you send to us, including text messages
  • Payment information processed through third-party services (e.g., TurboTenant, Stripe)

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to rental inquiries and schedule showings
  • Process rental applications and screen applicants
  • Communicate lease terms, move-in details, and maintenance updates
  • Send rent reminders and payment confirmations
  • Comply with Washington State landlord-tenant law requirements

SMS / Text Messaging

By providing your phone number and consenting to receive text messages from Triton Property Group LLC, you agree to receive SMS communications related to your rental inquiry or tenancy. These messages may include showing confirmations, lease updates, maintenance notifications, and rent reminders.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out of text messages. Reply HELP for assistance.

We do not sell or share your phone number with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers that assist with tenant screening (TransUnion), lease signing (DocuSign), and payment processing (TurboTenant, Stripe)
  • Legal or regulatory authorities when required by law

Data Retention

We retain tenant and applicant records for the period required by Washington State law, generally seven years from the end of a tenancy.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us at tritonpropertygroupseattle@gmail.com.
